## Motivation
- IMPAQTO’s 10 years of work generated **scattered quantitative metrics** (capital access, clients, jobs, NPS) and **qualitative feedback** (interviews, surveys, chatbot).  
- Leadership needed a clear activity → outcome → impact link under the **Theory of Change**.  
- Goal: **Turn fragmented data into actionable insights** for strategy and accountability.  

---

## Contribution
- Harmonized historical datasets across **Labs, Capital, and Coworking** programs.  
- Built a **ToC–Dashboard mapping** to connect indicators with outcomes.  
- Applied **chatbot text analysis** (5,000+ entries) to extract recurring themes.  
- Produced an **Institutional Narrative (2014–2024)** combining metrics and stories.  

---

## Method
- **Data cleaning & structuring**: Mapped ToC nodes → KPIs → dataset fields to ensure traceability.  
- **Text analysis (Python)**: Topic modeling, sentiment classification, keyword clustering, thematic dictionaries.  
- **Visualization & tools**: Multi-dimensional **Tableau dashboards** for capital access, client acquisition, job creation, and NPS.  

---

## Results
- Produced **reliable baselines**: 88 validated records with traceable activity → outcome links.  
- Drafted **8 ToC Outcome Narratives**, e.g.:  
  - 23 ventures accessed flexible financing  
  - 190+ jobs created  
  - 70%+ acquired new clients  
  - 92.4% ventures remained active post-program  
- Delivered an **Institutional Narrative (2014–2024)** aligning quantitative + qualitative evidence with global evaluation standards.  

📊 Value: Enhanced **monitoring, evaluation, and learning (MEL)** capacity; positioned IMPAQTO as a **regional impact leader** with evidence-backed storytelling.
